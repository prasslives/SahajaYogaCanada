Method

How these dates were worked out

Tithi comes from the Moon's ecliptic longitude minus the Sun's, so no ayanamsa is involved and there is nothing to calibrate. Lunar months are named by the sign the Sun enters during them, which correctly produces the leap months Adhika Jyeshtha 2026 and Adhika Chaitra 2029 — festivals skip those, as tradition requires.

Festivals are not all placed by sunrise. Each is reckoned by its traditional window: Mahashivaratri and Janmashtami at nishita (midnight), Ganesha Chaturthi and Ugadi at madhyahna (midday), Dussehra and Maha Navami at aparahna (afternoon), Diwali at pradosh (evening). Using sunrise for all of them put six festivals a day late.

Generated from the app's own calendar engine. Every date here is stored as an ordinary editable entry — anything wrong can be corrected in the admin panel without touching code.
